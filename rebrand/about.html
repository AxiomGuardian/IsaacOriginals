About

Isaac Rodriguez.

Systems architect. I look at how something is put together and ask what shouldn't be there.

For a long time I chased success, money, and what looked impressive on the outside. I failed. I got lost. I made mistakes. I ran from God more times than I can count. Through the pain and the wandering He has been patiently bringing me back to simple, consistent obedience. Not perfection. Not a platform. Faithfulness with what is in front of me today.

I am wired to build. Christ tells me who I am and everything else is context.

I document publicly because I have noticed two things about myself. When no one is watching, I drift. When I pretend, I rot. This site exists to fix both. The work exists because Christ put real problems in front of me and there is no spiritual reason to dodge them.

I am not a pastor. I am not a guru. I am not selling a system. I am one young man trying to be obedient with what has been put in his hands. If any of this serves you, praise God. If it doesn't, also praise God.
