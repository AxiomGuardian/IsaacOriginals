About

Isaac Rodriguez.

Systems architect. The job is looking at how something was put together and finding the part that should not be there.

Nobody actually wants more software. They want the thing to work once, without a workaround, without a second login, without somebody in another department retyping what was already typed.

There was a long stretch of chasing whatever looks impressive from the outside. Some of it worked. None of it filled the thing it promised to fill. That part is worth handing to somebody else, because it saves years: the win you are counting on to change how you feel is not going to change how you feel.

Christ settles who I am. Everything after that is context, this page included.

Not a pastor. Not a guru. Not selling a system. If any of it is useful to you, take it and go build something.
