The Journey

Three seasons.

Not a highlight reel. What actually happened, in the order it happened.

2021 to 2024

Learning and breaking.

Cadet years. Uniforms, encampments, early mornings, gear on the pavement before sunrise. The first real discipline I had came from that.

Alongside it I chased success, money and approval. I failed. I got lost. I made mistakes. None of it filled the hole it promised to fill.

2025

Discipline and return.

The habits came back before the feeling did. Most of it was unremarkable. Getting up. Doing the thing I said I would do. Praying when I did not want to.

Nobody was watching this part, which is probably why it held.

2026 to now

Execution.

Knowing what to do and not doing it was always the real problem. This season is just closing that gap. Four things being built. None of them finished.

Some mornings the fuel tank reads empty and the only honest prayer available is "God, I don't feel like this." He meets that one every time. Obedience is not a vibe. It is showing up anyway.
