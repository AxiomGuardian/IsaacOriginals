Kingdom Initiative Technologies

Technology bent toward the Church.

The enemy has used technology to disciple a generation into anxiety, comparison and isolation. The Church does not need to hide from the tools. She needs saints willing to rebuild them.

K.I.T. is my attempt at that. A slow, prayerful body of work. Real systems for real believers, rooted in Scripture and built to last. Every tool points back to the local church. Every line of code is stewardship.

“For God is not a God of disorder but of peace.” 1 Corinthians 14:33
